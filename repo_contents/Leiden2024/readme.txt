# Project Name
Churn_insurances_2024

### Model objective: 
The model objective is to build a customer churn prediction. Churn refers to when a customer cancels a previously purchased product or service contract. 
We are going to predict churn for our Damage Department based on historical data. Our Damage Department includes products such as home and travel insurance. 
We want to calculate the likelihood of churn for the coming year.

### Available/Used Data
Internal data on customer base and policies
External sociodemo data on zipcode level
